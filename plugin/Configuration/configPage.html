GlassJelly

Liquid Glass theme for the web client. Changes apply the next time a browser loads Jellyfin (reload the page; on phones close and reopen the app).

Off = Jellyfin's own look, without uninstalling the plugin.
The glass triangle in the header, on the startup screen and as the browser tab icon.
The art of what you are looking at, blurred, behind the whole interface.
Turns on backdrops once per device for users who never chose; their own setting is kept.
The glass under the mouse is lit from where the pointer is; no hover flashes while scrolling.
Docks the Skip Intro button into the glass control bar while it is visible.
Subtitles rise above the player controls while they are visible, and Settings → Subtitles → Subtitle Appearance applies to every subtitle type (ASS/SSA too). Sets every user's subtitle styling to "Custom". Fine-tune per device in the browser console: localStorage.jfSubGap (px above the bar, default 20) and localStorage.jfSubRest (px when the bar is hidden, default -1).
